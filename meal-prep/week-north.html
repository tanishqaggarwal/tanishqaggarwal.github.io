This week's three dishes

Every batch below is sized to exactly what the menu eats, plus one spare portion. Scale with a kitchen scale when you can.

Rajma Masala

5 plates this week · ~900 g batch
~197 kcal / serving ~170 g on the plate

Beans

  • 275 g dry rajma, soaked 8 hrs (or 2 × 400 g cans, drained ~550 g)
  • — water to cover by 5 cm for pressure-cooking
  • ½ tsp salt (for cooking water)

Masala base

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped (~200 g)
  • 2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, puréed or finely chopped (~250 g)
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½–¾ tsp red chili powder (to taste)
  • 1 tsp rajma masala or garam masala
  • ½ tsp amchur (or 2 tsp lemon juice)
  • ¾ tsp salt (to taste)
  • 150 ml water, plus more as needed

Method

  1. Drain soaked rajma. Pressure-cook with fresh water + salt until very soft (25–35 min / 8–10 whistles). Reserve ~½ cup cooking liquid.
  2. Heat oil. Cook onion 8–10 min until deep golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic 1 min, then tomato. Cook 6–8 min until oil separates.
  4. Stir in the powdered spices 30 sec. Add 150 ml water; simmer 2 min.
  5. Add drained rajma + a splash of cooking liquid. Mash ~¼ of the beans against the pot for body.
  6. Simmer uncovered 12–15 min until the gravy coats a spoon. Adjust salt.

Half the old batch — 275 g dry rajma, not 450 g. Canned: ~550 g drained beans, skip the long cook. Batch ~900 g — that is 5 plates of 170 g plus about 50 g spare.

Mixed Dal (with Spinach)

5 plates this week · ~1.05 kg batch
~176 kcal / serving ~200 g on the plate

Dal (pressure cooker)

  • 125 g yellow moong dal
  • 125 g masoor dal
  • 750 ml water (about 3 cups)
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ¾ tsp salt

Temper / masala

  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped (~125 g)
  • 2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped (~125 g)
  • 1 green chili, slit
  • ¼ tsp red chili powder (optional)

Finish

  • 100 g spinach leaves, washed & roughly chopped

Method

  1. Rinse the dals until the water runs clear. Pressure-cook with water, turmeric and salt: 3–4 whistles / ~12 min. Mash lightly — keep it thick.
  2. Heat oil. Add cumin until it sizzles (~20 sec).
  3. Add onion; cook 5–6 min until golden. Stir in ginger-garlic and chili 1 min.
  4. Add tomato (+ chili powder). Cook 4 min until soft and jammy.
  5. Pour the cooked dal in. Simmer 5 min, adding a splash of water only if too thick.
  6. Stir in the spinach until wilted (1–2 min). Taste for salt.

Reheat with 2 tbsp water per portion. Batch ~1.05 kg — that is 5 plates of 200 g plus about 50 g spare.

Paneer Bhurji

4 plates this week · ~400 g batch
~184 kcal / serving ~95 g on the plate

Mix in the pan

  • 200 g paneer, crumbled into pea-sized bits
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped (~125 g)
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped (~125 g)
  • 2 tsp ginger, minced
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp chopped cilantro (optional finish)

Method

  1. Heat oil. Bloom the cumin seeds 20 sec.
  2. Add onion + chili; cook 5 min until soft and lightly browned.
  3. Add ginger 30 sec, then tomato + turmeric + salt. Cook 4 min until the tomato softens and the moisture drops.
  4. Add the crumbled paneer + garam masala. Stir-fry 5 min on medium — don't dry it to dust.
  5. Off the heat: cilantro.

The densest dish in the rotation — 95 g on the plate is a full protein serving. Batch ~400 g — that is 4 plates of 95 g plus about 20 g spare.

Simple Sautéed Spinach

Side · 4 sides · ~300 g leaves
~40 kcal / serving ~75 g cooked

In the pan

  • 300 g spinach leaves (about 1½ bunches, tough stems removed)
  • 4 tsp oil (1 tsp per serving)
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 green chili, slit (optional)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ¼ tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cumin 15 sec, then garlic (+ chili) until just golden — don't burn.
  2. Add the spinach in handfuls with turmeric and salt. Toss 2–3 min until wilted and most of the water evaporates.
  3. Make it fresh on the night — it takes three minutes and doesn't batch well.

Made to order, not batched on Sunday.

Sunday prep

About 60–75 minutes active for three dishes. Do the Saturday-night soaks so Sunday is mostly cook and portion.

Saturday night

~5 min
  1. Soak 275 g rajma, 8 hrs.
  2. Optional: wash the salad veg and store it dry.

Phase 1 — Start the long cooks (in parallel)

~10 min to start · 30–45 min unattended
  1. Rajma — pressure-cook 25–35 min until very soft.
  2. Mixed dal — pressure-cook 12 min with turmeric + salt.
  3. Put 550 g dry quinoa or rice on (≈ 1.7 kg cooked). The week needs 120 g cooked grain per plate.
  4. While the pots run → Phase 2. Don't stand around.

Phase 2 — One chop, one base

~15–20 min
  1. Chop once for all three dishes: 3 onions, 4 tomatoes, ginger-garlic, chilies, cilantro.
  2. Prep the paneer: 200 g for the paneer bhurji. Keep it chilled.
  3. With only three dishes you can build one onion-tomato base and split it — it saves a pan and 15 minutes.

Phase 3 — Finish the three dishes

~25–35 min
  1. Rajma: half the masala base + spices + beans, simmer 15–20 min. ~900 g batch · 170 g portions.
  2. Mixed dal: temper cumin/onion/tomato, fold into the dal, wilt in the spinach. ~1.05 kg batch · 200 g portions.
  3. Paneer bhurji: onion → tomato → spices → 200 g crumbled paneer, 5–7 min. ~400 g batch · 95 g portions.

Phase 4 — Portion & store

~15 min
  1. Cool everything uncovered 10–15 min — trapped steam makes watery fridge food.
  2. Box by meal at the halved plate size: 95–200 g curry + 110–120 g grain + 100–150 g yogurt. Label the boxes.
  3. Fourteen plates come off three batches (900 g + 1.05 kg + 400 g), so nothing sits in the fridge past Sunday.
  4. Chop salad veg for 2–3 days only; do the rest mid-week. Portion fruit for breakfasts.

Efficiency rule: never cook one pot at a time. The long cooks and the grain should all be running before you start the masala. Three dishes means one chopping board, one base, and one wash-up.

Daily tips

Reheat Splash of water on the curries. Weigh after reheating if you are tracking tightly.
Protein rule Legume or paneer dish + yogurt on every plate. 100 g Greek yogurt ≈ 10 g protein.
Half-plate template 95–200 g curry + 110–120 g grain + 100–150 g yogurt. That is the whole plan.
Still hungry? Add salad and yogurt volume before you add grain — it costs the fewest calories.
