Core recipes

Batch these on Sunday. Reheat with a splash of water.

Mixed Dal (with Spinach)

6–8 servings

1½–2 cups moong + masoor · 1 onion · 2 tomatoes · ginger-garlic · turmeric, cumin, salt, chili · ½–1 bunch spinach

Pressure-cook dal with turmeric + salt. Temper cumin, onion, ginger-garlic, tomato. Add dal, simmer. Stir in spinach to wilt.

Rajma Masala

6 servings

Cooked rajma · onion-tomato base · rajma/garam masala · cumin · coriander · amchur · salt

Combine cooked rajma with masala base and spices. Simmer 15–20 min until thick and saucy.

Chana Masala

6 servings

Cooked kabuli chana · onion-tomato base · chana masala powder · cumin · coriander · salt

Same method as rajma — use cooked chana + chana masala powder. Simmer until flavorful.

Paneer Bhurji

Half of your paneer batch

300–400 g crumbled paneer · 1–2 onions · 2 tomatoes · chili · ginger · turmeric, cumin, garam masala, salt

Sauté onion + chili + ginger → tomato + spices → paneer. Cook 5–7 min.

High-Protein Khichdi

Sat (or Sun) dinner

1 cup mixed dal · ½–¾ cup quinoa/rice · onion · tomato · ginger-garlic · turmeric, cumin, salt · ½ bunch spinach

Pressure-cook dal + grain. Temper and mix in. Wilt spinach. Serve with Greek yogurt.

Simple Sautéed Spinach

Quick side · Tue / Wed / Fri

Spinach · 1 tsp oil · cumin · garlic/chili · salt · turmeric

Oil → cumin → garlic/chili → spinach + salt + turmeric. Wilt 2–3 min.

Grab-and-go breakfasts

Pick one every morning. Keep the yogurt portion generous for protein.

Night before · best option

Overnight Oats

  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 150–200 g Greek yogurt
  • ½–¾ cup milk or water
  • 1 fruit + optional chana/nuts

Night: mix oats + yogurt + liquid. Morning: fruit + toppings.

1–2 minutes

Greek Yogurt Jar

  • 200–250 g Greek yogurt
  • 1–2 pieces fruit, chopped
  • Optional: roasted chana or nuts

Layer the night before or morning of. Ready in under 2 minutes.

Under 5 minutes

Quick Hot Oatmeal

  • ½ cup rolled oats + water/milk
  • Microwave 2–3 min
  • Stir in 100–150 g yogurt + fruit

Stovetop or microwave. Finish with yogurt for protein.

Sunday prep

About 2–2.5 hours. Store everything airtight.

  1. Quick-soak or pressure-cook rajma + chana (or use canned).
  2. Pressure-cook rajma and chana fully.
  3. Cook 3–4 cups mixed dal (moong + masoor). Keep thick.
  4. Make a large onion-tomato masala base.
  5. Crumble/cube all paneer → turn half into bhurji.
  6. Wash & chop spinach. Stir half into mixed dal (or save for sauté).
  7. Cook 4–5 cups quinoa or brown rice.
  8. Portion yogurt and chop remaining veggies. Store airtight.

Daily tips

Reheat Splash of water on curries — keeps them saucy.
Protein rule Legume dish + generous yogurt or paneer every meal.
Portions Keep dal / rajma / chana generous. Rotis optional — quinoa or rice is fine.
Spinach plan Half into mixed dal on prep day. Rest for quick sauté or khichdi.
