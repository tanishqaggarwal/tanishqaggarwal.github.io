**High-Protein Vegetarian Indian Meal Prep**  
Printable Guide • No Tofu • Simple Grab-and-Go Breakfasts

### Shopping List
| Category | Items |
|----------|-------|
| Produce | 6–8 onions, 8–10 tomatoes, 3–4 cucumbers, 2 large bunches spinach, ginger, 1 garlic head, 6–8 green chilies, 1 bunch cilantro, fruit for 7 days |
| Dairy | 600–700 g paneer, 1.5–2 kg Greek yogurt / hung curd |
| Legumes & Grains | 500 g yellow moong dal, 400–500 g masoor (or toor) dal, 400–500 g rajma, 400–500 g kabuli chana, 1 kg quinoa or brown rice, 500 g rolled oats |
| Snacks | 200–250 g roasted chana or peanuts, small pack almonds/nuts (optional) |
| Pantry | Turmeric, cumin, coriander powder, garam masala, salt, oil/ghee |

### Sunday Prep (2–2.5 hrs)
1. Quick-soak or pressure-cook rajma + chana (or use canned).  
2. Pressure-cook rajma and chana.  
3. Cook 3–4 cups mixed dal (moong + masoor). Keep thick.  
4. Make large onion-tomato masala base.  
5. Crumble/cube all paneer → turn half into Bhurji.  
6. Wash & chop spinach. Stir half into Mixed Dal (or save for sauté).  
7. Cook 4–5 cups quinoa or brown rice.  
8. Portion yogurt and chop remaining veggies. Store everything airtight.

### Core Recipes
**Mixed Dal (with Spinach)** – 6–8 servings  
1½–2 cups mixed moong + masoor • 1 onion • 2 tomatoes • ginger-garlic • turmeric, cumin, salt, chili • ½–1 bunch spinach  
Pressure-cook dal with turmeric + salt. Temper cumin + onion + ginger-garlic + tomato. Add dal, simmer. Stir in spinach to wilt.

**Rajma Masala** – 6 servings  
Cooked rajma + onion-tomato base + rajma/garam masala + cumin + coriander + amchur + salt. Simmer 15–20 min.

**Chana Masala** – 6 servings  
Same method as Rajma using cooked chana + chana masala powder.

**Paneer Bhurji**  
300–400 g crumbled paneer • 1–2 onions • 2 tomatoes • chili • ginger • turmeric, cumin, garam masala, salt  
Sauté onion + chili + ginger → add tomato + spices → add paneer → cook 5–7 min.

**High-Protein Khichdi**  
1 cup mixed dal + ½–¾ cup quinoa/rice • onion • tomato • ginger-garlic • turmeric, cumin, salt • ½ bunch spinach  
Pressure-cook dal + grain. Temper and mix in. Stir spinach to wilt. Serve with Greek yogurt.

**Simple Sautéed Spinach**  
1 tsp oil → cumin → garlic/chili → spinach + salt + turmeric. Wilt 2–3 min.

### Grab-and-Go Breakfasts (Very Quick)
These are designed to be ready in under 5 minutes or prepped the night before. High-protein and completely non-Indian.

**1. Overnight Oats (best make-ahead option)**  
Per jar/serving:  
- ½ cup rolled oats  
- 150–200 g Greek yogurt  
- ½–¾ cup milk or water  
- 1 fruit (banana, berries, apple, etc.)  
- Optional: 1 tbsp roasted chana, nuts, or seeds  

**Night before:** Mix oats + yogurt + liquid in a jar.  
**Morning:** Add fruit and optional toppings. Grab and go.

**2. Greek Yogurt Jar**  
- 200–250 g Greek yogurt  
- 1–2 pieces fruit (chopped)  
- Optional: small handful roasted chana or nuts  

Layer everything in a container the night before or morning of. Ready in 1–2 minutes.

**3. Quick Hot Oatmeal**  
- ½ cup rolled oats + water or milk  
- Microwave 2–3 minutes (or stovetop)  
- Stir in 100–150 g Greek yogurt + fruit  

Done in under 5 minutes total.

**Daily rule:** Choose one of the three every morning. Keep the Greek yogurt portion generous for protein.

### 7-Day Menu
| Day | Lunch | Dinner |
|-----|-------|--------|
| **Mon** | Rajma + quinoa + cucumber salad + yogurt | Mixed Dal (spinach) + 100 g paneer + roti/quinoa |
| **Tue** | Chana Masala + rice/quinoa + raita | Mixed Dal + sautéed spinach + roti/quinoa |
| **Wed** | Rajma + quinoa + salad + yogurt | Paneer Bhurji + large salad or sautéed spinach |
| **Thu** | Chana Masala + roti/quinoa + raita | Mixed Dal (spinach) + quinoa + yogurt |
| **Fri** | Rajma + rice/quinoa + salad + yogurt | Paneer Bhurji or remaining paneer + Mixed Dal + spinach |
| **Sat** | Leftover Chana/Rajma bowl + extra yogurt + salad | High-Protein Khichdi (with spinach) + big yogurt |
| **Sun** | Remaining batches or paneer + yogurt + salad | Light Mixed Dal or leftover Khichdi + yogurt |

**Snacks:** Roasted chana/peanuts, extra Greek yogurt, cucumber + yogurt.

### Spinach & Khichdi Notes
- **Spinach**: Half into Mixed Dal on prep day. Rest for quick sauté (Tue/Wed/Fri) or added to Khichdi.  
- **Khichdi**: Mainly Saturday dinner (or Sunday). Uses existing ingredients + yogurt on top.

### Daily Tips
- Reheat curries with a splash of water.  
- Always add generous Greek yogurt to lunch & dinner.  
- Protein rule: Legume dish + yogurt or paneer.  
- Keep dal/rajma/chana portions generous.  
- Rotis optional — quinoa or rice works fine.
