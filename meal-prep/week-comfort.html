Core recipes

Batch on Sunday. Exact mix amounts below — scale with a kitchen scale when you can.

Dal Makhani (home-style)

6 servings · ~1.6 kg batch
~340 kcal / serving ~250 g cooked

Beans (soak 8 hrs)

  • 300 g whole black urad dal
  • 50 g rajma
  • 1.4 L water for pressure-cooking
  • 1 tsp salt

Gravy

  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil + 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped (~200 g)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 300 g tomato purée (about 3 blended tomatoes)
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt (to taste)
  • 60 ml cream or 100 g Greek yogurt whisked (lighter option)
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi, crushed

Method

  1. Pressure-cook soaked urad + rajma with water and salt until very soft and creamy (often 40–50 min). Mash some against the pot.
  2. Heat ghee/butter. Onion 8 min golden → ginger-garlic 1 min → tomato purée + powders 8–10 min until thick and oil shows.
  3. Add cooked dal + a splash of water. Simmer low 20–30 min (longer = better), stirring so it doesn’t stick.
  4. Stir cream or yogurt off heat; kasuri methi + garam masala. Portion ~250 g. Lighter than restaurant — still rich.

High-protein comfort. Yogurt instead of lots of cream keeps kcal nearer ~340/serving.

Lauki Chana Dal

7 servings · ~1.8 kg batch
~230 kcal / serving ~250 g cooked

Dal + lauki

  • 300 g chana dal (about 1½ cups), soaked 30 min
  • 500 g lauki (bottle gourd), peeled, 2 cm cubes
  • 1 L water (about 4 cups)
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped

Tadka

  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 pinch hing (optional)
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 1–2 green chilies
  • ½ tsp red chili powder (optional)

Method

  1. Drain chana dal. Pressure-cook with lauki, water, turmeric, salt, tomato until dal soft and lauki tender (3–4 whistles).
  2. Whisk lightly — keep some texture. Adjust water for thick stew.
  3. Tadka: oil → cumin → hing → ginger + chili. Pour over dal; simmer 3 min. Portion ~250 g.

Light, high-volume North Indian dal. Lauki keeps calories modest.

Methi Paneer

6 servings · ~1.7 kg batch
~390 kcal / serving ~280 g cooked

Gravy

  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 large onions, sliced (~400 g)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 medium tomatoes, puréed (~400 g)
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1½ tsp cumin powder
  • 2 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1–1½ tsp salt
  • 120 ml water (½ cup)
  • 3 tbsp cream or 4 tbsp milk (optional)

Methi + paneer

  • 30 g kasuri methi (dried) — crush well; or 150 g fresh methi leaves, chopped
  • 400 g paneer, 2 cm cubes

Method

  1. If fresh methi: sauté chopped leaves 3–4 min in 1 tsp oil until aroma mellows; set aside. If kasuri: crush and keep ready.
  2. Heat oil. Onions 8–10 min deep golden → ginger-garlic 1 min → tomato purée + powders 8 min until thick.
  3. Add water + salt; simmer 2 min. Add methi; cook 2 min.
  4. Add paneer (+ cream/milk). Gentle simmer 5 min. Portion ~280 g (~65 g paneer).

No mushrooms. Kasuri methi is pantry-friendly; fresh methi is brighter if in season.

Aloo Matar

6 servings · ~1.5 kg batch
~160 kcal / serving ~250 g cooked

In the pan

  • 500 g potatoes, 2 cm cubes
  • 300 g green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped (~200 g)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped (~250 g)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1½ tsp coriander powder
  • ¾–1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 240 ml water (1 cup)

Method

  1. Heat oil; cumin 20 sec → onion 6 min → ginger-garlic 1 min → tomato + powders 5 min.
  2. Add potatoes; stir 2 min. Add water + salt; cover 10–12 min until potatoes almost done.
  3. Add peas; cook 5–6 min more. Garam masala. Portion ~250 g. Lighter sabzi — pair with dal/paneer.

~160 kcal per 250 g. Protein from the dal or paneer on the plate.

Aloo Gobi

6 sides · ~1.2 kg batch
~140 kcal / serving ~200 g cooked

In the pan

  • 500 g cauliflower florets (medium)
  • 300 g potatoes, 2 cm cubes
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 large onion, sliced (~200 g) optional
  • 1 tbsp ginger, julienned
  • 1–2 green chilies
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ¾ tsp amchur
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cilantro

Method

  1. Heat oil in a wide pan. Cumin → potato cubes 4–5 min to seal edges.
  2. Add cauliflower, onion (if using), ginger, chili, turmeric, coriander, salt. Toss well.
  3. Cover and cook on medium-low 12–15 min, stirring every few minutes — dry-ish, not steamed mush. Splash water only if sticking.
  4. Amchur + garam masala + cilantro. Portion ~200 g.

Dry North Indian sabzi. No coconut, no mushrooms.

Jeera Aloo (optional side)

4 sides if you need extra veg
~140 kcal / serving ~150 g cooked

Quick side

  • 500 g boiled potatoes, cubed
  • 1½ tbsp oil
  • 1½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp amchur

Method

  1. Cumin in hot oil → potatoes + turmeric + salt. Toss 5–7 min. Amchur. Portion ~150 g.

Emergency 10-minute side from boiled potatoes.

Grab-and-go breakfasts

Pick one every morning. Weights are as prepared in the jar/bowl.

Sunday prep

Aim for ~2.5 hours (dal makhani likes a longer simmer). Soak urad Saturday night — non-negotiable for creamy dal.

Saturday night

~5 min
  1. Soak 300 g whole black urad + 50 g rajma (8–12 hrs).
  2. Optional: soak 300 g chana dal 30 min right before cooking Sunday instead of overnight.

Phase 1 — Dal makhani first (longest item)

~10 min start · 40–50 min pressure + simmer later
  1. Drain urad/rajma; pressure-cook with water + salt until very soft and creamy (often 40–50 min total).
  2. Same window: start quinoa/rice and boil a big pot of potatoes (aloo matar + optional jeera aloo).
  3. If you have a second cooker: start chana dal + lauki once makhani is under pressure (or right after).
  4. While pressure cooks → Phase 2 chopping.

Phase 2 — Chop + paneer gravy mise en place

~20–25 min
  1. Chop: onions, tomatoes (or blend purée), lauki cubes, cauliflower florets, ginger-garlic, chilies.
  2. Cube 400–500 g paneer. Measure kasuri methi, peas, spices into small bowls.
  3. Build dal makhani gravy base (onion → tomato purée → spices) so it’s ready when beans are soft.

Phase 3 — Finish in this order

~50–60 min
  1. Dal makhani: beans + gravy → low simmer ≥20 min (30 better) → cream or whisked yogurt + kasuri methi. ~250 g portions.
  2. Lauki chana dal: cook through → tadka. ~250 g portions.
  3. Methi paneer: onion-tomato gravy → methi → paneer 5 min. ~280 g portions.
  4. Aloo matar: one pan, ~20 min. ~250 g portions.
  5. Aloo gobi: wide pan, dry-ish, 12–15 min — or save cauliflower for Wed so it stays firmer.
  6. Optional: toss leftover boiled potatoes into jeera aloo for a spare side.

Phase 4 — Portion

~10–15 min
  1. Cool dal makhani fully before lids (it thickens a lot cold — portion while warm-spoonable).
  2. Box: dals 250 g, paneer curry 280 g, sabzi 150–250 g, grain 150 g, yogurt 150–200 g.

Efficiency rule: Dal makhani is the critical path — start it first, simmer it while you cook every other dish. Aloo gobi is the best candidate to defer mid-week if you’re tired.

Daily tips

Dal makhani Low simmer = creaminess without endless cream.
Protein rule Dal + yogurt or paneer every meal. Sabzi fills the plate, not the protein quota.
Aloo gobi Wide pan + less water keeps it roasted, not steamed.
No mushrooms None in this week’s recipes.
