This week's three dishes

Every batch below is sized to exactly what the menu eats, plus one spare portion. Scale with a kitchen scale when you can.

Lauki Chana Dal

5 plates this week · ~1.05 kg batch
~184 kcal / serving ~200 g on the plate

Dal + lauki

  • 175 g chana dal, soaked 30 min
  • 275 g lauki (bottle gourd), peeled, 2 cm cubes
  • 575 ml water (about 2⅓ cups)
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 1 small tomato, chopped

Tadka

  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 pinch hing (optional)
  • 2 tsp ginger, minced
  • 1 green chili
  • ¼ tsp red chili powder (optional)

Method

  1. Drain the chana dal. Pressure-cook with lauki, water, turmeric, salt and tomato until the dal is soft and the lauki tender (3–4 whistles).
  2. Whisk lightly — keep some texture. Adjust water for a thick stew.
  3. Tadka: oil → cumin → hing → ginger + chili. Pour over the dal; simmer 3 min.

Lauki keeps the calories modest while the chana dal carries the protein. Batch ~1.05 kg — that is 5 plates of 200 g plus about 50 g spare.

Palak Paneer

5 plates this week · ~950 g batch
~229 kcal / serving ~180 g on the plate

Spinach purée

  • 275 g spinach leaves, stems trimmed
  • — boiling water to blanch + ice water to shock
  • 1 green chili (optional, blanched with the spinach)

Gravy base

  • 1 tbsp oil + 2 tsp butter/ghee (optional)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped (~125 g)
  • 2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped (~125 g)
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 75 ml water, as needed
  • 1 tbsp cream or 2 tbsp milk (optional, for colour)

Paneer

  • 185 g paneer, cut into 2 cm cubes
  • — optional: soak the cubes in warm salted water 10 min so they stay soft

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach 60–90 sec; ice bath; squeeze lightly; blend with the chili to a smooth purée.
  2. Heat oil. Cumin seeds 20 sec → onion 6 min golden → ginger-garlic 1 min → tomato + dry spices 5 min.
  3. Stir in the purée + 75 ml water. Simmer 4 min — don't boil hard or the colour dulls.
  4. Add the paneer cubes; simmer gently 4 min. Finish garam masala + cream/milk if using.

About 35 g paneer per plated serving, plus the yogurt alongside. Batch ~950 g — that is 5 plates of 180 g plus about 50 g spare.

Bhindi Masala

4 plates this week · ~650 g batch
~120 kcal / serving ~150 g on the plate

In the pan

  • 350 g bhindi (okra), washed, fully dried, 1.5 cm slices
  • 2 tbsp oil (bhindi needs a little more so it doesn't slime)
  • 1 medium onion, sliced (~125 g)
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped (~125 g)
  • 2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chili, slit
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ¾ tsp cumin powder
  • ¾ tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp amchur or chaat masala
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp garam masala

Method

  1. Pat the bhindi bone-dry. Heat 1½ tbsp oil in a wide pan; sauté the okra on medium-high 8–10 min until the edges brown and the stickiness goes. Remove.
  2. Add the rest of the oil. Onion 5 min → ginger-garlic + chili 1 min → tomato + all powders except garam masala. Cook 4 min until soft.
  3. Return the bhindi; toss 3 min. Finish with garam masala.

Dry the okra properly or it turns slimy. Re-crisp in a dry pan when reheating. Batch ~650 g — that is 4 plates of 150 g plus about 50 g spare.

Jeera Aloo (optional extra)

Side · 3 sides · ~375 g batch
~140 kcal / serving ~125 g cooked

In the pan

  • 350 g potatoes, boiled, peeled, 2 cm cubes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ¾ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 green chili, slit
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ⅓ tsp amchur
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tbsp cilantro, chopped

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes until just done; cube when cool enough to handle.
  2. Heat oil; cumin until it crackles. Chili 10 sec. Add potatoes + turmeric + salt + coriander.
  3. Toss on medium-high 5–7 min until the edges crisp slightly. Amchur + cilantro.

Not scheduled in the 7-day menu and not counted in the calorie totals — make it only if you want a fourth thing in the fridge, and skip the potatoes on the grocery list otherwise.

Sunday prep

About 60–75 minutes active for three dishes. Do the Saturday-night soaks so Sunday is mostly cook and portion.

Saturday night

~5 min
  1. Soak 175 g chana dal, 30 min (day-of is fine).
  2. Optional: wash the salad veg and store it dry.

Phase 1 — Start the long cooks (in parallel)

~10 min to start · 30–45 min unattended
  1. Chana dal + lauki — pressure-cook together, 3–4 whistles.
  2. Put 550 g dry quinoa or rice on (≈ 1.6 kg cooked). The week needs 120 g cooked grain per plate.
  3. While the pots run → Phase 2. Don't stand around.

Phase 2 — One chop, one base

~15–20 min
  1. Chop once for all three dishes: 2 onions, 3 tomatoes, ginger-garlic, chilies, cilantro.
  2. Prep the paneer: 185 g for the palak paneer, 160 g cubed for the veg plates. Keep it chilled.
  3. With only three dishes you can build one onion-tomato base and split it — it saves a pan and 15 minutes.

Phase 3 — Finish the three dishes

~25–35 min
  1. Lauki chana dal: whisk lightly, pour over a cumin-ginger tadka. ~1.05 kg batch · 200 g portions.
  2. Palak paneer: blanch and blend the spinach, build the base, fold in purée then paneer. ~950 g batch · 180 g portions.
  3. Bhindi masala: brown the okra dry first, then fold it back into the onion-tomato masala. ~650 g batch · 150 g portions.

Phase 4 — Portion & store

~15 min
  1. Cool everything uncovered 10–15 min — trapped steam makes watery fridge food.
  2. Box by meal at the halved plate size: 150–200 g curry + 110–120 g grain + 100–150 g yogurt. Label the boxes.
  3. Fourteen plates come off three batches (1.05 kg + 950 g + 650 g), so nothing sits in the fridge past Sunday.
  4. Chop salad veg for 2–3 days only; do the rest mid-week. Portion fruit for breakfasts.

Efficiency rule: never cook one pot at a time. The long cooks and the grain should all be running before you start the masala. Three dishes means one chopping board, one base, and one wash-up.

Daily tips

Reheat Splash of water on the curries. Weigh after reheating if you are tracking tightly.
Protein rule Legume or paneer dish + yogurt on every plate. 100 g Greek yogurt ≈ 10 g protein.
Half-plate template 150–200 g curry + 110–120 g grain + 100–150 g yogurt. That is the whole plan.
Still hungry? Add salad and yogurt volume before you add grain — it costs the fewest calories.
